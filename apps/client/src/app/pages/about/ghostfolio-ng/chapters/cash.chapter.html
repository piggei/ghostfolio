Cash Management

Cash Management records cash entering, leaving and moving between accounts alongside investment activities. Cash and holdings are distinct: positions represent investments, while total portfolio wealth also includes the cash that you choose to include.

Accounts and cash balances

  1. Open Accounts, create or select an account with a currency, then open its details and Cash Balances. Creating an account alone does not create a deposit.
  2. For a new account, enter the first deposit on its actual date. For an older account, review the existing balances and reconcile to a known balance on a known date. Opening the view alone does not activate a historical account’s cash ledger; saving a movement or preference does. Existing balance snapshots are retained as dated reconciliations when management starts.
  3. Review the two independent preferences: Update Cash Balance by default for new activities, and Consider this account’s cash in analyses and allocations. Check Update Cash Balance on each financial activity; changing the default is not a replay of every past activity.

Deposits, withdrawals and reconciliation

  1. In Cash Balances, select the date and operation, enter the amount and any note, and choose Insert. The account determines the currency. Deposits and withdrawals require positive amounts; the application subtracts withdrawals automatically. Future dates are not accepted.
  2. For a deposit, cash increases by amount minus fee. For a withdrawal, cash decreases by amount plus fee. Enter a non-negative fee in the account currency. Financial activity-linked amounts already include their fees: do not add the same fee again as a manual cash movement.
  3. Choose Reconciliation and enter the actual cash balance, including zero or a negative balance. Its fee must be zero. It replaces the calculated balance at that point in the journal; it is not an extra deposit and does not reconstruct missing earlier transactions. Later movements continue from the reconciled balance.
  4. Review the running balance in the journal. Use the movement’s Actions menu to edit or delete a manual entry. For linked investment movements, open and correct the original financial activity. A newly negative or worsened negative balance requires confirmation; review the warning rather than using reconciliation to conceal an error.

Transfers between accounts

From Accounts, choose Transfer Cash Balance, select From and To, enter a positive Value in the source account’s currency and confirm Transfer. The transfer uses today’s date. For different currencies, the application converts the destination amount with the available exchange rate for today; this dialog has no historical date or custom destination-amount control. Review both balances afterward.

A transfer between your accounts is an internal movement, not a new deposit from outside your portfolio. Managed accounts retain linked counterpart entries; legacy accounts remain supported. Transfer entries cannot be edited or deleted separately: use a new reverse transfer if needed. This version has no separate source-fee or destination-fee fields in the transfer dialog; do not assume a bank’s two fees have been recorded automatically.

Cash is not investment profit

Deposits, withdrawals and reconciliations change recorded wealth, not investment gains. Base-currency cash is outside the investment-return boundary; foreign-currency cash can contribute currency effects. Cash visibility and inclusion settings are separate from the selected return method. Cash fees reduce cash and appear in fee totals, but the ROI/ROAI investment-only result excludes standalone fee activities: the change in total wealth need not equal the investment-only result.

Example in EUR: deposit 1,000 with a fee of 2 → cash 998; withdraw 100 with a fee of 1 → cash 897; reconcile to the bank’s actual 880 → cash 880, not 1,777. A later internal transfer of 200 to another EUR account leaves 680 here and adds 200 there. With no transfer fees or exchange effects, the transfer itself leaves total wealth unchanged. None of these amounts is an investment gain.

Cash CSV import and export

  1. Use Export CSV in Cash Balances for the whole account journal. To import, use Import CSV there or on Accounts, select the destination account or create a new one, and inspect the preview. Previewing and cancelling do not create an account or save movements.
  2. Required columns: Date, Operation, Amount, Currency. Optional: Fee, Sequence, Note, Reference. Use UTF-8, comma or semicolon separators, YYYY-MM-DD dates, a decimal point and no thousands separator. The currency must match the destination account: CSV import does not exchange currencies. Italian column headings are accepted too.
  3. For hand-written files use DEPOSIT, WITHDRAWAL or RECONCILIATION. Deposit and withdrawal amounts are positive; reconciliation uses the actual balance and Fee 0. Supply Sequence to order same-day movements, especially around a reconciliation. The example below ends at 880 EUR. 
Date;Operation;Amount;Currency;Fee;Sequence;Reference
2026-10-01;DEPOSIT;1000;EUR;2;1;opening-1
2026-10-02;WITHDRAWAL;100;EUR;1;2;withdrawal-1
2026-10-03;RECONCILIATION;880;EUR;0;3;balance-1

Check new and existing rows, final and minimum balances, currency and warnings before confirming Insert movements. An identical Reference in the same account is skipped; changed contents under the same reference are a conflict. Possible duplicates without references need review. Errors block the whole import; existing entries are not overwritten. If the journal changes after previewing, preview again.

Cash CSV does not create financial activities. Import those first, then match linked cash rows to the correct existing activities; ambiguous matches need your selection. Keep exported metadata and linked-activity columns unchanged. Files are limited to 2 MB and 5,000 records including metadata. A new transfer cannot be reconstructed from one side alone: use the complete JSON restore for both accounts. Cash CSV is not a full portfolio backup. Linked amounts are already net and are not taxed again. Identical stored historical rows can be recognized as existing, but new rows must match the current activity’s net cash amount. Use full JSON restore to preserve older journals whose stored amounts predate the tax correction.
