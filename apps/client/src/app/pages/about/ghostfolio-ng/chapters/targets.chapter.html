Portfolio Targets

Open Portfolio → Targets to compare your current asset allocation with a desired allocation. Choose one of five editable presets or configure your own targets.

Configure the target percentages and permitted deviation, then save. The target configuration is global: selecting an individual account changes the comparison, not the saved targets.

The portfolio value, total allocation drift, maximum deviation and allocation chart refer to the same selected portfolio or account. Review the asset-class details to identify differences from your targets and values outside tolerance.

Targets respects excluded accounts and Ghostfolio-NG cash inclusion preferences. Eligible account cash is reconciled separately from securities to avoid double-counting it. Cash excluded from analysis is not added to the allocation.

Missing or inconsistent financial data can make the comparison unavailable. This long-only version does not support included negative cash exposures, short positions or liabilities. Portfolio Targets provides analysis only: it does not execute trades, automatically rebalance or provide investment recommendations.
