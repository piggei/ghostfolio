Profit and Loss Calendar

Find the calendar on Home → Overview, outside Zen Mode. It loads historical performance independently of the main overview chart’s selected range. It helps you inspect profitable and unprofitable periods without treating cash deposits as profits.

  1. Choose Daily or Weekly to inspect the selected month, Monthly for the selected year, or Last 30 days for the current rolling window. Previous and Next move one month, or one year in Monthly view. Last 30 days resets to the current date and has no historical-period arrows. Weeks run Monday to Sunday and weekly totals use only the portion inside the selected month.
  2. Where your permissions and view mode allow amounts, switch between the base-currency amount and %. Restricted or hidden-value views do not expose monetary amounts. Positive and negative values use different colors and signed numbers; a dash means unavailable, not zero.

What the amounts mean

Each day’s monetary PnL is the difference between two consecutive calendar dates’ cumulative net performance including currency effects. Weekly and monthly amounts sum those daily differences. A gap in historical dates is not assigned entirely to the next day; incomplete elapsed daily data can make period totals unavailable. Non-trading days are included when valuations are available and may still reflect income, costs or currency changes.

The % view requests a separate ROAI calculation for each period; it is not the daily monetary PnL divided by yesterday’s balance, nor the change in a cumulative percentage. In this version it is available only when ROAI is selected. With ROI, TWR or MWR, percentage cells may show a dash even when monetary PnL is available. Percentages must not be added to obtain a monthly return.

The calendar reuses net investment performance: contributions themselves are not gains, investment dividends and applicable investment fees/taxes affect net amounts, and foreign-currency conversion affects the currency-adjusted series. It is not a bank-account balance calendar or a complete change-in-wealth statement; the cash and standalone-fee boundaries described in Cash Management still apply.

Example: consecutive cumulative net results of 0, 12, 7 and 10 EUR produce daily PnL of +12, −5 and +3 EUR, totaling +10 EUR. A missing observation between 12 and 7 does not justify labelling −5 as a one-day result. A deposit of 500 EUR alone is not +500 EUR of PnL.
