Other Ghostfolio-NG Improvements

Complete portfolio restore

  1. On the source instance, open Settings → Export Data and keep the JSON backup private. On the destination, use Import Data beside Export Data, or Accounts → Import JSON.
  2. For each source account, choose a new account, an existing compatible account or Exclude. Names are not matched automatically. To restore a cash ledger use a new or empty account, or a compatible account with its financial activities already imported and no existing ledger. Include both accounts of every restored transfer.
  3. Review the preview and warnings before Import Data. Accounts, activities, exported custom-instrument data, tags, platforms and cash history are restored together; an error rolls back the restore. Existing data is retained. A complete JSON restore already includes cash, so do not import the same cash CSV again.

Older exports cannot reconstruct cash history or activity links they never contained. Unmanaged legacy accounts remain unmanaged. Login credentials and server configuration are not transferred; base currency and performance method can optionally be restored only into an empty portfolio. Shared manual instruments without an owner can lack metadata or historical prices in the upstream export: verify them afterward.

Remaining cost basis after partial sales

Realized performance uses the remaining cost basis when you sell part of a holding and later buy more. Example without fees: buy 10 at 100, sell 5 at 110, buy 5 at 200, then sell 10 at 210. Gross realized gain is 50 + 600 = 650. Previously sold quantities must not dilute the average cost of the remaining position. Review the holding’s activity history when reconciling this result with broker records.

Same-day capital reuse

ROI also recognizes capital invested and released before the end of a day. Buy and sell 1,000 on the same day and the capital is not zero. If you then reinvest 1,100 that day, the same-day peak is 1,100, not the sum 2,100. Dates and transaction order matter; enter the real activity sequence instead of changing dates to force a desired return.

Editable fee descriptions

In Activities, edit a standalone Fee activity to correct its name. A shared or administrator-owned instrument profile is separated when necessary so renaming your fee does not rename other activities. This changes the description, not the fee amount; check both fields before saving.
