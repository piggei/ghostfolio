Tax Management

Record actual tax amounts separately from investment values and fees. This feature tracks taxes you enter; it does not calculate tax rates, choose a jurisdiction, generate a tax return or file it for you.

  1. Open Activities and create or edit a Buy, Sell, Dividend or Interest activity. Enter its date, account, instrument or currency, quantity and unit price as usual, then enter the monetary Tax amount separately from Fee. Tax uses the currency displayed beside the field; it is an amount, not a percentage.
  2. When Update Cash Balance is enabled, a buy debits gross value plus fee and tax; a sell, dividend or interest activity credits gross value minus fee and tax. The combined net amount is calculated in the activity currency, then converted to the account currency at the activity date. There is one linked movement, not a separate tax debit. Previously saved movements keep their recorded amounts: reading, editing only a note or restoring a JSON backup does not repair earlier tax omissions. An explicit financial edit recalculates the linked amount; review any manual corrections you previously entered before changing historical activities.
  3. Review Tax in the activities table and edit the original activity to correct the amount, date, currency or account. Do not duplicate the same tax as a standalone fee or cash withdrawal. JSON import/export preserves tax; activity CSV import accepts Tax and Withholding Tax columns. Older entries without a tax amount use zero.

Tax reduces monetary net investment performance while gross performance and gross dividend/interest amounts remain distinct. ROAI and ROI use this net result. The current TWR and MWR percentage routines have no explicit tax adjustment: do not interpret those percentages as after-tax returns. Use ROAI or ROI when reviewing the net result after recorded taxes.

Example: a new dividend of 100 EUR with Tax 26 EUR and Fee 0 remains a gross dividend of 100 EUR and credits 74 EUR of linked cash. Tax remains recorded separately as 26 EUR. Changing Tax to 20 updates the same linked movement to 80 EUR without another tax debit. Deleting the activity removes its linked cash effect. Monetary net investment performance already deducts the recorded tax; do not change the gross dividend to 74 or enter the same tax as another cash withdrawal.

Recorded tax information is not a substitute for professional tax advice. Check your records against broker statements and consult a qualified adviser for your situation.
